About

I test systems until they can be trusted, and think about the business they’re built to serve.

I studied Information Management at St. Xavier’s College: half computer science, half management. Now I’m doing an MBA. The pairing is deliberate.

Software rarely fails for purely technical reasons. More often it fails in the gap between what was built and what the business needed. I like working in that gap: reading a requirement as a set of test cases, and reading a defect report as a business risk.

How I work

  1. 01
    Verify, then trust. Every claim a system makes is a test case waiting to be written.
  2. 02
    Think in systems. Features don’t fail alone. Data, flows and people fail together.
  3. 03
    Speak both languages. Turn defects into business risk, and goals into acceptance criteria.
